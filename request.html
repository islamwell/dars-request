🌿 Questions

Gently answered

How far in advance should I send a request?+

As early as you can. A few weeks’ notice gives the team the best chance of fitting your date. Adding an alternate date also helps a lot.

When will I hear back?+

Once the team has reviewed your request, you’ll be contacted on the phone or WhatsApp number you provided, with an approval or alternate options.

Can the lecture be recorded?+

Only with the Ustaza’s prior permission. Choose your preference in the Sound & Tech step and the team will confirm it with you.

Is my information saved if I close the page?+

Yes. Your draft is saved privately in this browser on this device, so you can come back and finish later.

What if my plans change?+

Contact the team as soon as possible and quote your request reference number. You’ll get it after you submit.
